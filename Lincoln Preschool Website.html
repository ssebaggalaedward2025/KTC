Our story

Lincoln Preschool is a nursery school in Kampala, Uganda. We give every child a gentle start to learning, with caring teachers, safe spaces and a daily routine that builds confidence.

Mission

To nurture every child with love and quality early learning, so they are ready for primary school and for life.

Vision

Children who are confident, curious and kind, with a brighter future ahead of them.

Approach

Learning through play, songs, stories and hands-on activities, at each child’s own pace.

A day at Lincoln

A calm, predictable routine helps young children feel secure.

  • MorningArrival, greetings and free play
  • Mid-morningCircle time, songs and rhymes
  • Late morningLearning activities: letters, numbers, art
  • MiddaySnack or lunch, hand washing
  • AfternoonRest time, then outdoor play
  • Home timeStory, tidy up and safe pick-up
